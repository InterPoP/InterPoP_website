FROM FILES TO FINDINGS

Your literature, made easier to navigate.

InterPoP brings importing, visual exploration, filtering, and organisation into one focused workspace.

01

Import your library

Bring in Zotero BibTeX exports, folders containing BibTeX and PDFs, or individual PDF files.

02

Explore relationships

Connect papers by content, shared authors, or references they have in common in an interactive graph.

03

Understand the collection

See topic clusters, publication timelines, and journal rankings without leaving your library.

04

Organise your way

Add tags and custom columns, save category networks, search full text, and filter your paper table.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your library stays on your Mac.

InterPoP stores your papers, extracted text, tags, and saved networks locally. There is no account to create and your library is not uploaded.
